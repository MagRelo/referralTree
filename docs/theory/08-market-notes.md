## Implication for MagRelo

On-chain multilevel **graph primitives** are still a tiny, under-measured niche. Demand lives in attribution, anti-farm, partner payouts, and agent-readable opportunity indexes. Wedge: settlement-truth / geometric path-credit layer next to quest and affiliate ops — not "take Layer3's ARR."
